## Crypto steel: debunking the "steel" myth to be equipped with the best crypto steel wallet

Metal devices made to store your seed phrase and backup your crypto wallets are often called "steel crypto wallets". However, it's crucial to discern that the best material is not just any standard steel. Instead, the mandatory superior choice for your metal crypto wallet lies in stainless steel or titanium.

Their resistance to corrosion is higher than most other materials. This property is essential for the long-term storage and legibility of your seed phrase. A well-chosen metal serves as the first line of defence in safeguarding your digital wealth from rust and environmental damages.

## The melting point and why it matters for your seed phrase storage

The melting point is a very important factor when choosing the metal of your mnemonic seed phrase storage. There are many other influencing factors to consider.

In case of a house fire average temperatures can reach up to 1000°C (1832°F), with even higher °T bursts. In such cases, the integrity of your metal seed is compromised if it's made of a low-melting-point metal. Ideally, the metal should have a melting point at least 250°C (480°F) above the average house fire temperature.

## Resistance to corrosion: your crypto seed storage must be immune

When discussing metal seed backups, another characteristic often overlooked is corrosion resistance. It is crucial to select a metal that can resist corrosion to ensure the longevity of your metal seed wallet. Corrosion mainly comes from water, humidity and acidic environments

A crypto seed storage made of a metal susceptible to corrosion can gradually deteriorate over time when exposed to saline environments. It could result in the potential loss of your seed phrase valuable information. This is particularly true for people living in coastal areas, where the air is constantly charged with micro drops of saline water.

## Engraving ease: don't choose a hard metal for your seed phrase storage

While high resistance to heat and corrosion are crucial, another equally important factor is the ease of engraving. Certain metals, while offering excellent thermal and corrosion resistance, might be too hard. It makes the engraving process of your wallet seed phrase too difficult.

The best hardness for a stainless steel plate is usually soft to mild. The 316L variant is an ideal choice, as it provides a good balance of resistance and engraving ease. In this regard, pure titanium is also a top performer to keep your seed phrase secure.
